Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping and selling on {{ SITE_NAME }}.

🛒 Buying & Shopping

How do I place an order?

Simply browse products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can shop from multiple sellers in a single transaction.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal. All payments are processed securely.

How is shipping handled?

Each seller handles their own shipping. You'll receive tracking information for each order. Standard shipping is KSh4.99 per seller order.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel orders that haven't been shipped yet. Go to your order history and select the order you want to cancel.

What is your return policy?

Return policies vary by seller. Check the product page or contact the seller directly for their specific return policy.

🏪 Selling & Store Management

How do I become a seller?

Click "Become a Seller" on the homepage or go to your profile and select "Start selling." Complete the onboarding form and wait for approval.

What fees do you charge?

We charge an 8% commission on each sale. This is deducted from your earnings automatically.

When do I get paid?

Payouts are processed monthly. You can view your earnings in the seller dashboard.

How do I manage my inventory?

Use the seller dashboard to add, edit, and manage your products. You'll receive alerts when stock is low.

🔐 Account & Security

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email. We'll send you a link to reset your password.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption to protect your payment information. We never store your full credit card details.

Can I have both buyer and seller accounts?

Yes! You can shop as a buyer and also run a seller store with the same account. Just complete the seller onboarding to activate your store.
